- [ ] Check confirmed Playing XI.

- [ ] Check if Bumrah, Boult, Shami, Mohsin are playing.
- [ ] Check pitch report.
- [ ] Check dew possibility.
- [ ] Do not bet full wallet.

### After Toss

- [ ] If MI bats first, MI total markets become more useful.
- [ ] If LSG bats first, LSG powerplay markets become more useful.
- [ ] If heavy dew expected, chasing team gets advantage.
- [ ] Avoid toss/match combo unless you already know toss result.

### During First 2 Overs

- [ ] 0 wickets + 15–20 runs = Over side stronger.
- [ ] 1 wicket + below 12 runs = Avoid Over.
- [ ] Swing/seam visible = Under side stronger.
- [ ] Batting looks easy = Over side stronger.

### During Powerplay

- [ ] 50+ runs and 0–1 wickets = Team total Over possible.
- [ ] 40 or less and 2+ wickets = Team total Under better.
- [ ] Strong batter still set = wait, don't rush.

### After 10 Overs

- [ ] 85+ with 7+ wickets = 180–200 possible.
- [ ] 75 or less with 3+ wickets = Under safer.
- [ ] Pant/Pooran/SKY/Rickelton set = death-over explosion possible.
